Traditional Bilona Method Ghee

HoofBeats stands for honest dairy made with care, bringing together traditional wisdom and modern hygiene. Our GIR cows are nurtured in a calm farm environment, helping us create milk, ghee, curd, and paneer with a naturally rich taste.

From slow Bilona-style ghee making to careful daily handling, we focus on purity at every step. The result is dairy that feels close to home, trusted by families who value clean ingredients and authentic Indian flavor.
